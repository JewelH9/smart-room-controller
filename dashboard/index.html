What the occupancy model has learned
Green bar = pushes the prediction towards "occupied", red = towards "empty".
Both models train on your data while this page is open and are stored in this browser. They learn from a 10-minute-ahead answer to each snapshot taken every minute: predict first, then compare with what really happened, then update. The simple guesses ("same as now", "no change") are hard to beat at 10 minutes, so the scoreboard tells you honestly when the model has not earned its place yet.
